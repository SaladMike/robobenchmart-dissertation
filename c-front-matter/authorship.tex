\section*{Authorship Attribution Statement}

This dissertation reports original research carried out by the candidate. The research design, implementation, experiments, analysis and dissertation writing are the candidate's own work. Third-party models, software libraries, datasets and published methods used in the study are acknowledged through the relevant citations and licence terms.

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

The candidate's contributions consist of the following work:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.15\baselineskip]
\item A dissertation project implementing retail mobile manipulation in ManiSkill3: simulator and robot configuration, asset curation, procedural store-plan and shelf generation, policy-server deployment and evaluation-client integration.
\item The inference and evaluation pipeline for vision-language-action policies, covering five atomic task families in seven task instances and two composite tasks decomposed by an oracle.
\item Adaptation and evaluation of four policy families under the Pose, Layout and Pairing conditions and the Seeds control, at 100 trials per task--item--fixture triplet.
\item The demonstration pipeline: anchor-based collection with screw-motion planning and an RRT-Connect fallback, acceptance testing of every rollout, and 2,976 episodes and 1,401,169 transitions stored with the seeds needed to reconstruct them.
\item Failure-mode analysis of a reported set of 2,906 unsuccessful episodes under an earliest-primary rule across eleven pipeline-ordered categories, together with the author's reproduction and documentation of each retained protocol discrepancy following a separate peer code reading.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

All work claimed as a contribution in this dissertation was carried out by the candidate. Contributions originating from cited third parties are not claimed as the candidate's own work.
