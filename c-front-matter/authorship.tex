\section*{Authorship Attribution Statement}

This dissertation reports original research carried out by the candidate. The research design, implementation, experiments, analysis and dissertation writing are the candidate's own work. Third-party models, software libraries, datasets and published methods used in the study are acknowledged through the relevant citations and licence terms.

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

The candidate's contributions consist of the following work:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.15\baselineskip]
\item Construction of a reproducible mobile-manipulation evaluation environment for retail scenarios, including ManiSkill simulator configuration, asset acquisition, scene generation, model-service deployment and end-to-end integration of the evaluation client.
\item Design and implementation of the inference and evaluation pipeline for vision-language-action (VLA) policies across five atomic task families and two composite tasks, covering pick-and-place and open/close manipulation.
\item Adaptation and comparative evaluation of four VLA policy families across training scenes, randomised initial poses, held-out test scenes and unseen task--item pairings.
\item Construction of a data pipeline spanning motion-planning trajectory collection, raw trajectory replay with visual observation rendering, and conversion from HDF5 trajectories to the RLDS format.
\item Failure-mode analysis of VLA policies in retail mobile manipulation, addressing long-horizon action stability, cross-scene generalisation, target-interaction precision and task-decomposition capability.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

All work claimed as a contribution in this dissertation was carried out by the candidate. Contributions originating from cited third parties are not claimed as the candidate's own work.
