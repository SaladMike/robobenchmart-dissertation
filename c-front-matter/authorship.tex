\section*{Authorship Attribution Statement}

This dissertation reports research carried out by the candidate, reproducing, configuring, and empirically evaluating the RoboBenchMart benchmark in ManiSkill3. The experimental execution, independent 42-configuration evaluation design, empirical analysis of released Octo and $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoints, protocol auditing, and dissertation writing are the candidate's own work. Upstream benchmark components, simulation assets, public model checkpoints, and software libraries are acknowledged through citations and relevant licensing terms.

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

The candidate's contributions consist of the following work:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.15\baselineskip]
\item Reproduction, deployment, and verification of the RoboBenchMart simulation environment in ManiSkill3, including procedural scene loading and runtime client fixes.
\item Design and execution of an independent multi-tier evaluation protocol across 42 atomic task configurations under Seeds, Pose, Layout, and Pairing distribution shifts.
\item Rigorous empirical evaluation of two released vision--language--action checkpoints (Octo and $\pi_{0.5}$), completing 1,260 evaluated episodes per model (2,520 episodes total) with full logging and video artifact indexing.
\item Systematic comparative analysis contrasting the operational dynamics of continuous hinge articulation (door opening and closing) against dense, high-clutter pick-and-place manipulation.
\item Forensic protocol audit identifying critical runtime mechanisms that constrain physical policy success, including open-loop 50-step action chunking, immobilized torso kinematics, and scene reuse in door layout conditions.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

All work claimed as a contribution in this dissertation was carried out by the candidate. Contributions originating from cited third parties and the upstream RoboBenchMart project are acknowledged and not claimed as the candidate's own work.
