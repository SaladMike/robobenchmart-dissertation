\section*{Authorship Attribution Statement}

This dissertation reports an independent re-evaluation, carried out by the candidate, of two policy checkpoints released with the RoboBenchMart benchmark. The RoboBenchMart benchmark, including its store generator, asset library, robot configuration, tasks, success predicates, demonstration data, evaluation protocol and failure taxonomy, was created by its authors at emb-ai and is described in arXiv:2511.10276. The Octo and $\pi_{0.5}$ checkpoints evaluated here were fine-tuned and published by the same authors; the candidate did not train or fine-tune any model. ManiSkill3, OpenPI, Octo and the other software used are third-party work, acknowledged through citations and licence terms.

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

The candidate's contributions consist of the following work:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.15\baselineskip]
\item Setting up the public RoboBenchMart code, scenes and checkpoints, and repairing the evaluation path: restoring a missing import in the floor environment, adapting the evaluation client to gymnasium wrappers, adding connection timeouts, and giving each Octo server connection its own policy instance.
\item Running the 42 atomic evaluation configurations for both checkpoints (2,520 episodes), including serial resumption after failures and parallel execution across several servers and clients.
\item Archiving the results: per-configuration and per-episode tables, logs, videos and SHA-256 manifests.
\item A trial run of the upstream scene generator and an overview-camera recording tool.
\item Analysis of the results, examination of the evaluation code and version history, and the writing of this dissertation.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}

All work claimed as a contribution in this dissertation was carried out by the candidate. Work originating from the RoboBenchMart authors and other cited third parties is acknowledged and not claimed as the candidate's own.
